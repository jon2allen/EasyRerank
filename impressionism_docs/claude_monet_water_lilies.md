# Water Lilies (Nymphéas)

**Artist:** Claude Monet  
**Location:** Musée de l'Orangerie, Paris, and various museums worldwide  

### Overview and Imagery
The *Water Lilies* series comprises approximately 250 oil paintings by Claude Monet, depicting his flower garden at his home in Giverny, France. They were the main focus of his artistic production during the last thirty years of his life, many painted while he suffered from cataracts.

### Visual Details
The paintings depict the surface of the pond, filled with clusters of green lily pads and blooming water lilies. Monet dissolves the horizon line, showing only the water's surface, reflecting clouds, willow trees, and sky. The colors are lush and varied, ranging from soft pinks and whites of the lilies to deep purples, blues, and emerald greens of the water and reflections. The brushstrokes are thick and gestural, creating a dreamlike, almost abstract visual field.

### Significance
Monet’s late works represent a transition from Impressionism toward pure Abstraction. He wanted the paintings to create the illusion of an endless whole, a refuge of peaceful meditation.
