# The Boulevard Montmartre at Night (1897)

**Artist:** Camille Pissarro  
**Location:** National Gallery, London  

### Overview and Imagery
*The Boulevard Montmartre at Night* is a celebrated night landscape painted by Camille Pissarro toward the end of his life. After renting a room overlooking the grand boulevards of Paris, Pissarro painted the same street view multiple times under different times of day, weather, and seasons.

### Visual Details
The painting captures the bustling energy of the Paris boulevard after dark. Pissarro paints the wet, rain-soaked pavement reflecting the bright yellow glow of gas and electric streetlamps, store windows, and carriage lights. The dark outlines of carriages and crowds of pedestrians create a sense of constant movement. Pissarro’s brushwork is thick and painterly, focusing on the atmospheric haze and glare of lights rather than sharp details.

### Significance
This painting is an outstanding example of Impressionist interest in nocturnal lighting and urban modernization. It highlights the transformation of Paris into the "City of Light" during the late 19th century.
